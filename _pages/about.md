* Greetings! I’m a Software Development Engineer at Microsoft, Azure Networking Team. 

* I received my Master’s Degree in Electrical Engineering at [Nagoya University](https://www.nagoya-u.ac.jp/), advised by Prof. [Hiroshi Hasegawa](https://www.nuee.nagoya-u.ac.jp/labs/satolab/people/index.html). I received my Bachelor’s Degree in Electronic and Intformation Engineering at [Xi’an University of Technology](https://www.xaut.edu.cn/), advised by Dr. [Qing Liu](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Qing-Liu-122).

* My research interests lie in Networking and Reinforcement Learning. Specifically, I’m focusing on applying learning techniques to improve the efficiency of resource allocation in optical networking system. I also had a little work on Evolutionary Computation, specifically, genetic algorithm.
